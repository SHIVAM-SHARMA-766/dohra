Settings

Every recording

  • Audio and video, at the best quality your screen allows.
  • A transcript, made on this computer by the companion while you record.
  • Spoken language detected automatically, line by line, so Hindi, English and Hinglish can mix freely.
  • Your microphone, unless you allow switching it off below.

Microphone

Off: your microphone is recorded every time and the popup does not ask. On: the popup shows "Include my microphone", and you can untick it for a recording.

Downloads copy

The copy survives removing the extension. Without it, the library inside the browser is the only copy.

Speech model

Used for every transcript. Switching applies to everything not transcribed yet, including a class in progress.
