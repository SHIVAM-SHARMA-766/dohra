Settings

Dohra picks the best format by itself. These are the few things you can change.

Every recording

Always the same, so nothing to choose before class.

  • Audio and video at the best quality your screen allows
  • Your microphone, unless you allow switching it off below
  • A transcript in Hinglish, if you want one: Hindi in Roman script ("theek hai"), English as English, with names from the meeting
  • Private: everything stays on this computer

Transcripts

Made on this computer by the companion. Turn them off to use Dohra as a plain recorder.

Speech model

A switch applies to everything not transcribed yet, even a class in progress.
