Welcome to Dohra

Record your online classes on this computer, with a Hinglish transcript you can search later. Nothing is uploaded. Three quick steps, once.

Full-quality video Hinglish transcript Stays on your computer

  1. Allow the microphone

    So your own questions and answers are in the recording too.

  2. Install the companion

    A small program (about 40 MB) that makes transcripts on this computer with whisper.cpp. The browser starts it when needed; no audio leaves the machine.

    Checking…

    Run these in a terminal. They need the GitHub CLI signed in to an account with access to the Dohra repository. This page notices by itself when it is done.

  3. Choose a speech model

    The model turns speech into text. Bigger models understand Hindi and Hinglish better but need more time. You can download another one, or switch, any time in Dohra's Settings.

    Available once the companion is installed.

You're all set

Open your class in a tab and click the Dohra icon, or press Alt+Shift+R. Recordings and transcripts appear in the Library.
